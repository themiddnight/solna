not. This page explains the difference.

marks that means and how you may use them.

- say truthfully that your project is "based on Solna" or "a fork of Solna", as plain text

- contribute to this repository. Your pull request does not use the brand, it improves it.

## If you fork and publish

Before you deploy a fork, rename it and replace the brand:

- the app name and descriptions in `index.html`, `public/assets/site.webmanifest` and `metadata.json`
- every image under `public/assets/`
- the in-app About text and the murva link in `src/components/settings/AppModal.tsx`
- the canonical and Open Graph URLs in `index.html`, which point at the official deployment

Questions, or want permission for something not listed here? Open an issue.
